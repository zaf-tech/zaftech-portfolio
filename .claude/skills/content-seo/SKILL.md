# Content SEO

Use this skill to grow Zaftech's authority around enterprise AI topics that match its services.

## Workflow
1. Review existing blog and service coverage.
2. Group target topics into clusters.
3. Find missing commercial-support and informational-support content.
4. Map each suggested article or FAQ to a money page or province page.
5. Output a prioritized backlog in `_workspace/01_content-seo-backlog.md` and internal-link plan in `_workspace/02_internal-link-plan.md`.

## High-fit clusters
- enterprise AI consulting Canada
- private LLM deployment
- agentic AI for operations
- AI compliance and governance
- AI for finance, healthcare, legal, logistics, and professional services

## Guardrails
- Prefer depth over volume.
- Avoid creating multiple pages for the same intent without clear differentiation.
- Tie content ideas to business value, not just traffic.
