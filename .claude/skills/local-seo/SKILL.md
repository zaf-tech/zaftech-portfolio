# Local SEO

Use this skill for Zaftech's Canada-focused regional search strategy.

## Workflow
1. Inspect province pages and the province data model.
2. Check whether each page has distinct industry framing, trust cues, and service relevance.
3. Suggest improvements to metadata, body copy, FAQs, internal links, and schema opportunities.
4. Write prioritized changes to `_workspace/02_province-page-plan.md`.

## What good looks like
- province pages are distinct, not spun duplicates
- Canadian terms and compliance cues are used naturally
- local pages support service pages rather than compete blindly with them

## Guardrails
- Do not invent offices, reviews, or local certifications.
- Keep claims aligned with real service coverage.
